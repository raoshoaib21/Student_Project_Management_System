About Us

Built to keep student projects on track.

Student PMS is a focused workspace for final-year students and their supervisors — plan the work, share the evidence, and stay in step week by week.

Our purpose

Managing a final-year project is full of moving pieces: scope, tasks, documents, weekly progress and supervisor feedback. We built Student PMS so that all of that lives in one place — no more scattered emails, lost files, or unclear expectations.

Students get a clear plan and a running record of what they've delivered. Supervisors get visibility into each project and a simple way to review progress and respond with feedback.
